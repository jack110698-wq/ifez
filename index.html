About IFEZ

A center of business innovation created by global companies and talents

A special economic zone attracting foreign investment and building an international business environment centered around Incheon’s Songdo, Yeongjong, and Cheongna.

Mission

Innovation · Creativity · Social Responsibility

Advance a sustainable, high-value ecosystem by connecting industry, government, academia and research—while enhancing quality of life and global competitiveness.

4 Missions & Strategies

Focus areas to accelerate the next decade

Concise, actionable pillars designed for global audiences and policy stakeholders.

1) Driving Sustainable Growth Momentum

Scale investment, jobs, and export capacity while maintaining fiscal and environmental sustainability.

2) Reinventing the Investment Attraction Strategy

From incentives to outcomes: target high-impact sectors (bio, AI, green tech) with partnership-based pipelines.

3) Tailored Regional Specialization

Differentiate Songdo · Yeongjong · Cheongna with distinct roles to avoid overlap and maximize synergy.

4) Regulatory Innovation for Global Competitiveness

Streamline permits, pilot sandboxes, and align standards with global best practices.
